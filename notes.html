Learned HTML Tags

Here are 5 useful HTML tags I have learned during my coursework:

<article>
Specifies self-contained content that could be independently distributed or reused.
<abbr>
Represents an abbreviation or acronym, often used with a title attribute to show the full expansion.
<blockquote>
Indicates a section that is quoted from another source.
<sup>
Defines superscript text, which is displayed half a character above the normal line.
<cite>
Used to reference the title of a creative work or the author of a quote.
